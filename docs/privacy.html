Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 (added the App Store check page). Covers Raptr Agent Builder, Raptr Agent Finder and DOGG World Check.

These ChatGPT apps are run by Wildhaven Homes LLC. This policy covers what happens to information the app receives.

What the app receives

Only what ChatGPT sends to the app's tools: a search phrase, an agent name, an operating system choice, or the text of an agent file to check. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, account or payment details.

What we do with it

We use it only to answer that request, in memory, and then discard it. We do not store it in a database, use it to train models, sell it, or share it, and we do not track you across sessions.

Requests you choose to send

If you ask for something the apps can't do yet and agree to pass the request on, we keep the text of that request, with no name or contact details, to decide what to build next.

Service providers

The app runs on Microsoft Azure. Azure keeps short-lived technical logs, such as request time, status and IP address, which we use only to keep the service running, under the Microsoft privacy statement. These logs do not include what you send to the app. We also count which tool was used and whether it worked, so we know what to improve; those counts never include your words, files or data. Agent searches read the public open agent registry (RAR) on GitHub. Your use of ChatGPT is covered by OpenAI's own privacy policy.

The agent store and paid calls (api.airaptr.com)

Text sent to the store (for example an App Store rejection message) is used only to answer that request and is not stored. Paid calls are paid in USDC on the Base network through the x402 protocol; Coinbase's payment facilitator verifies and settles them. For paid calls we keep the payment receipt (amount, network, the paying wallet address and the transaction) for our books. A wallet address is public on the blockchain; we do not link it to a name, and we do not sell or share it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

The App Store check page (airaptr.github.io/appstore)

The listing or rejection text you paste is sent to api.airaptr.com to answer that request and is not stored. If you press "I want this", we keep which product you asked for and, only if you typed one, your email address, in our own database (Microsoft Azure, not public). The address is used for one message when card checkout opens and is then deleted, or deleted after 90 days at the latest, whichever comes first. We do not use it for anything else or share it. To have it deleted sooner, open an issue or use the contact page.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue.
