ParkVerdict privacy policy

ParkVerdict collects nothing. The sign is read on your iPhone, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

The camera photo, or the photo you choose, is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. ParkVerdict doesn't save it, upload it or look at any other photo in your library.

Settings and reminders

Your permit zone, placard setting and reminder time are kept only on your iPhone. Reminders are ordinary notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There's no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests. The only links (this page and the support page) open in Safari.

Third parties

None. No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.

Keeping and deleting data

Nothing is retained by us. To delete everything ParkVerdict keeps on your phone, delete the app.

Children

ParkVerdict collects no information from anyone, including children.
