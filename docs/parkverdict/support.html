ParkVerdict support

Need help, or found a sign it reads wrong? Email wildhavenhomesllc@gmail.com. A person reads every message. A photo of the sign helps.

Checking a sign

Tap Check a sign and take a photo of the whole sign, straight on, with every panel in the picture. Or tap Choose a photo to use one you already took. You get the answer for right now, the rules in plain words, and the words ParkVerdict read.

"Check the sign yourself"

ParkVerdict says this when part of the sign couldn't be read, or says something it doesn't understand (like valet, school days or events). It never guesses yes. Try a closer photo, or tap Fix the words and type what the sign says.

Reminders

When you can park for a limited time, tap Remind me. The first time, your iPhone asks to allow notifications. Change how early you're reminded in Settings (the gear).

Permits and placards

In Settings, add your residential permit zone or turn on "I have a disability parking placard". Signs that let permit or placard holders park will then say yes for you.

Trying it without a sign

Tap Try a sample sign on the first screen.

ParkVerdict helps you read signs; it doesn't replace them. Always read the sign yourself too. Holidays, events and temporary signs can change what's allowed. Privacy policy
