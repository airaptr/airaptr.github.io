This is a free public demo. It runs on free AI models that can be wrong, and your messages are sent to third-party model providers who may store them. Please don't share personal, financial or login details.
More about this demo
  • Answers are general information only, not legal, medical, tax or investment advice.
  • No account and no cookies. We don't keep your conversation; it lives in this page until you close it.
  • The demo can only use three public tools: find ready-made AI agents, check business names and domains, and fetch live world numbers. It can't buy, send or change anything.
  • Limited to 20 messages an hour per visitor, so everyone gets a turn.
  • Raptr is open source (MIT). Run it yourself with your own key and model: github.com/airaptr/raptr.
