Family Story Book

Sit down with Grandma, open ChatGPT, and let it ask the questions. It saves her stories in her own words, lets the whole family add theirs, writes the chapters only from what was said, and lays it all out as a real 6×9 book.

How it works

  1. Start the book. Ask ChatGPT (or Claude) to start a Family Story Book about the person. You get a private book key and a family key.
  2. Interview. One gentle question at a time: childhood, love, work, hard times, what they want you to know.
  3. Invite the family. Share the family key. Brothers, sisters and cousins add their own memories from their own chat.
  4. Write and preview. Chapters are written from the saved memories only, never invented. Then you get a print-ready PDF with photo and memory pages.

Your family's stories stay yours

Books are private: only someone with a key can see or add to them. Keep the book key to yourself; it can edit or delete the book. Deleting a book removes its memories, chapters and previews. We never use your stories for anything else.
