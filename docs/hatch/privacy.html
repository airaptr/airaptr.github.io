Hatch privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026. Hatch is made by Wildhaven Homes LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Hatch app for iPhone.

The short version: we don't collect your data. Hatch talks only to your own computers.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no servers that receive anything from Hatch, no accounts and no sign-in. Hatch contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and we use no data brokers.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • Your messages, approvals and status go directly from your phone to your own computer running Hatch Brainstem, at the address you set, over your own private network (Tailscale). They never pass through us, and we cannot see them.
  • Your voice stays on your phone. Hatch listens only while you talk with it. Apple's speech recognition turns what you say into text on the iPhone itself; only that text goes to your own computer, like a typed message. No recording is kept or sent anywhere. Replies are read aloud by the iPhone's own voice.
  • Siri. When you ask Siri ("Talk to Hatch", "What's waiting in Hatch", "Stop everything in Hatch"), Siri hands Hatch your words; Hatch sends them only to your own computer and gives Siri the answer to say.
  • Eggs you choose to open in Hatch are sent to your own computer, which shows you what the egg is and hatches it only if you say so. Nothing from an egg runs on your phone.

Who else is involved

No third party receives your data from us, because we receive none. Two services you choose yourself are involved: Apple (speech recognition on the device, Siri, and the Hatch Estate subscription, which Apple bills and manages; we get no payment details or purchase history) and Tailscale (the private network you install and sign into to reach your computers). Their own privacy policies cover them.

What is kept on your phone, how long, and how to delete it

Hatch keeps only its settings (your estate's address, whether Demo mode is on, whether replies are read aloud) and your phone's own side of the conversation (your name and the phone's, if you gave them, and what you and the phone said to each other). It stays on your phone until you delete it, and never leaves the phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. What your computers keep is on your computers, under your control.

Children

Hatch is not directed at children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and the App Store privacy details with it.
