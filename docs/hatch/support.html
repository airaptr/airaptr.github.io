Need help? Email wildhavenhomesllc@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

What you need

  • Hatch Brainstem running on at least one of your computers.
  • Tailscale on that computer and on your iPhone, signed into the same account.
  • The Hatch app on your iPhone. Just looking? Gear → Demo mode shows Hatch with sample data; nothing is sent anywhere.

"This phone isn't on your estate yet."

Open Tailscale on your iPhone, sign in with the same account as your computer, make sure it says Connected, then come back to Hatch and tap Try again.

"Can't reach your estate right now."

Check that Tailscale is connected on your iPhone and that your computer is awake. Then tap Try again.

Changing which computer Hatch talks to

Tap the gear → Estate address → type your computer's address → Check → Use this address.

Stopping everything

Tap the red STOP button, then press and hold the big button for one second, or say "Stop everything", or tell Siri "Stop everything in Hatch". Everything pauses until you tap Start again on the Status tab.

Opening an egg

Tap the egg file in Messages or Files → Share → Hatch. You'll see what it is in plain words. Tap "Hatch it" to let your computer hatch it into a room of its own, or "Not now".

The Hatch Estate subscription

Using your own estate from your phone is Hatch Estate: free for 7 days, then weekly (the price in your currency is shown in the app). Cancel anytime in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. STOP, what's waiting, approvals and Demo mode are always free. Terms of Use (EULA) · Privacy policy
