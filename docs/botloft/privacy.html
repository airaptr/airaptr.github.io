Botloft privacy policy

Botloft collects nothing. The app connects only to the Botloft server you choose and, through it, to the AI model provider you set up. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Your server holds your conversations

Your account, bots, conversations, files and routines live on the Botloft server you connect to: one you run yourself, or one someone gives you access to. Whoever runs that server is responsible for that data. We don't run it and can't see it.

Your AI provider

When a bot works, your server sends what it needs to the AI model provider you chose, under that provider's own privacy terms. Botloft doesn't add anything of its own.

On your Mac

The app keeps the address of your server and its sign-in on your Mac. Files you drop on Botloft go only to the chat you have open. Notifications are ordinary Mac notifications.

No ads, analytics or tracking

There's no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

If you email us

Writing to us is optional. If you do, we receive what you send, including your email address, and use it only to reply. We don't share it or add you to any list. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Third parties

We share nothing with third parties, because we collect nothing. Your server and AI provider are services you choose.

Children

Botloft isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
