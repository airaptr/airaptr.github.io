Getting started

Botloft is the Mac app for a Botloft server. On first launch, enter your server's address (it starts with https://), then sign in. Your server's owner can give you the address.

Ask from anywhere

Click the Botloft icon in the menu bar, or press Control-Option-B in any app. A small chat drops down; press Escape or click elsewhere to hide it. Right-click the icon for more.

Hand over files

Drop files on Botloft in the Dock or on the menu bar icon. They're added to the chat you have open, up to 10 files of 10 MB each.

Answer from a notification

Allow Botloft's notifications when your Mac asks. When a bot finishes, reply right in the notification.

Something's wrong

"Could not reconnect to the saved server": check that the server is running and that its address is right, then try again from the start screen.
A bot doesn't answer: the AI model provider set up on your server may be out of credit or misconfigured. Check it in Settings.
