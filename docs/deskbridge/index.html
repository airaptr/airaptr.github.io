See and control every computer you own, from your phone.

DeskBridge shows the live screen of each Mac and Windows PC you own. Tap to click, type, and switch or close windows, over your home Wi-Fi or private VPN.

What you need: the DeskBridge server running on a computer you control, and the DeskBridge app on your iPhone or iPad. Sample Data (Demo Mode) in the app works with nothing installed.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no ads and no cloud relay. The app asks before it connects for the first time, then talks only to the server on your own computer. Read the privacy policy.
