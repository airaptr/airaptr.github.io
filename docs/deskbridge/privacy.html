DeskBridge privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026. DeskBridge is made by Wildhaven Homes LLC ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. DeskBridge does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, screen images, keystrokes or device identifiers. There is no account and no sign-in.

What the app sends, and to whom

Before the first connection, the app asks your permission. Once you allow it, the app sends your taps, typed text, keys and window commands directly from your device to the DeskBridge server at the address you enter, which runs on your own computer, over your Wi-Fi or private VPN. That computer sends back its screen picture and list of windows. None of this passes through us.

Third parties

No third party receives any data from DeskBridge. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and uses no AI service.

What is stored, and how to delete it

The app keeps three things on your device only: the server address you entered, the pairing code shown by your computer (in the device's secure Keychain, which does not sync to iCloud), and whether you allowed the connection. They stay until you tap Forget This Computer under Connection, or delete the app. We keep no data, because we receive none.

Children

DeskBridge is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
