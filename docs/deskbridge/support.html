Getting started

  1. On a computer you control, start the DeskBridge server on its private VPN or home Wi-Fi address, for example deskbridge serve --host 100.101.102.103. Only do this on a network you trust: anyone who can reach that address can see and control your computers.
  2. Open DeskBridge on your iPhone or iPad. Read the first screen, then tap Allow Connection.
  3. Tap Connection and enter your computer's address on your Wi-Fi or VPN, for example http://192.168.1.20:7844.
  4. Tap any computer to see its screen, tap to click, type text, or focus and close windows.

Questions

Can I try it without a server?
Yes. Tap Use Sample Data (Demo) on the first screen, or turn on Show Sample Data (Demo Mode) under Connection.

Does DeskBridge send my screens to the cloud?
No. The app connects only to the server address you enter, on your own network. Nothing is sent to us.

The app can't reach my computer.
Check that the server is running, that your phone is on the same Wi-Fi or VPN, and that the address and port are right. iOS may ask for permission to use the local network; allow it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

How do I control an ultrawide monitor?
Open a computer and tap 2× Ultrawide under the screen to pan across it.

How do I stop the app from connecting?
Under Connection, turn off Allow Connection to My Computers. The app saves nothing, so closing it also resets the connection.
